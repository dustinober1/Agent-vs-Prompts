# Testing and Verification

This document details the methods used to verify and validate the "Stop Prompt Engineering" project's content and structure.

## Automated Verification
There are currently no automated linting, spellchecking, or link-checking tools (e.g., `markdownlint`, `vale`, `linkchecker`) configured for this repository. 

## Manual Verification Methods

### Structural Validation
Chapters are manually checked against `chapters/_chapter_TEMPLATE.md` to ensure they contain all the necessary sections (Purpose, Reader Takeaway, Key Points, Draft, etc.).

### Content Continuity
The two core case studies (Research+Write and Instructional Design) are used throughout the chapters to ensure consistent application of the concepts.

## Future Testing Strategies
The book itself identifies several "verification" topics that should eventually be applied as part of the project's own development cycle:
- **Evals and Golden Sets:** (Ref: Chapter 18) Development of a set of benchmark responses and criteria to ensure content quality and clarity.
- **Link Verification:** Automated checking for internal and external references.
- **Linting:** Integration of Markdown linting to enforce formatting standards.
